Discover Matka
✦ Born from Indian Earth

The art of
drinking water.

Before refrigerators, filters and machines, there was the Matka — an Indian earthen pot that naturally keeps water cool, fresh and deeply connected to the earth.

∞

For generations, Indian homes have relied on clay pots to store drinking water. The practice is simple, sustainable and beautifully effective.

Crafted by earth.
Perfected by time.

A matka is more than a container. Its porous clay body allows tiny amounts of water to slowly evaporate from the surface.

This natural evaporation draws heat away from the water inside, keeping it pleasantly cool without electricity. No compressor. No plastic. No complicated technology.

Simple. Natural. Timeless.
